# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community welcoming, harassment-free,
and respectful regardless of experience, identity, or background.

## Our Standards

* Assume good intent.
* Be kind, direct, and concise.
* Stay on-topic in issues, PRs and review; disagreements about code are
  inevitable, but keep them productive.
* No harassment, hate speech or doxxing of any kind.
* Respect the time of maintainers and fellow contributors.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for moderating the project's spaces and may
remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code. A
permanent ban may follow repeated or severe violations.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to <security.rivid@vuce.in> (or any maintainer via GitHub).
Such reports will be handled with discretion; every complaint will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of
the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community impact is judged by maintainers' response at their discretion.

1. **Correction** — An inappropriate use of language: written warning.
2. **Warning** — A single incident or a series of actions: warning with
   consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary Ban** — A serious violation: temporary ban from interaction.
4. **Permanent Ban** — A pattern of violation or a single violation causing
   significant impact: permanent ban.
